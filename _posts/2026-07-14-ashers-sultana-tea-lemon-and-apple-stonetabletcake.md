### Ingredients
- 1 Lemon
- 4 Tbsp chia seeds
- 2 cups self raising flour
- 2 Cups water
- ½ cup sultanas
- 1 apple, skinned
- ½ Castor sugar or white sugar
- 1 Tbsp brown sugar
- ¼ Tsp Mixed spice
- 2 Tbsp Oil of any kind
- ½ oat milk
- 2 Tsp of english breakfast tea
- 2 tsp Vanilla extract

### Instructions
1. PREHEAT OVEN TO 180C
2. Add the water to a pot along with lemon zest, vanilla, lemon juice, and tea. Bring to a boil for about 20 minutes.
3. While boiling, dice sultanas and apple into small cubes, about ½ a cm2. Put into a small bowl and toss with allspice and brown sugar. Set aside.
4. Mix flour and sugar in a large bowl.
5. Mix flour and sugar in a large bowl.
6. Remove the lemon tea mixture from heat, and strain into a bowl.
7. Add 12 tbsp of this mixture to another bowl, then add 4 tbsp of chia seeds and let sit for five minutes.
8. Add oil, chia seed mixture, oat milk, to the dry ingredients and mix until combined. Then, add about ¼ of the remaining lemon tea solution and mix well. Add apple and sultana.
9. The final mixture should be a gelatinous swamp, viscosity of spit after skittles or like cupcake mixture. Add more lemon tea mix or flour to get this.
10. Spread on a baking tray lined with paper to desired thickness, I usually hit like ½cm but it’s whatever you want.
11. Bake in 180c oven for like 25 minutes, checking with a skewer every now and then because I really don’t know how long it should take.
